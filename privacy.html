Privacy Policy

Plank Challenge · Last updated 4 October 2026

Plank Challenge is a small app for holding a plank every day with friends. It is made by Leo Bacevicius ("we"). This page says what the app keeps about you, why, who else is involved, and how to delete it. The short version: we keep only what the app needs to work, we never sell it, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

What we keep

Your account. Your email address and how you sign in (email code, password, Sign in with Apple or Google). If you use Apple or Google, the name they share with us is suggested as your display name. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.
Your profile. Your display name, an optional profile photo, your time zone (so streaks follow your own days), the plank time you entered during set-up, and your notification settings.
Your planks. When each plank started and how long you held it. Your streaks and records are worked out from these.
Your groups. The groups you create or join, their names and photos, invitations you send or receive — including email addresses you invite — and your alerts (for example "Sam joined Core Crew").
Your phone's notification address. A token Apple gives the app so we can send you notifications. It identifies the app on your phone, not you.

We don't collect your location, contacts, health data, advertising identifiers or anything about how you use other apps.

Who can see what

  • People in your groups see your display name, photo and plank stats (streak, today's time, best time, totals).
  • Anyone on Plank Challenge can see your display name and photo in the list of people they can invite to a group. Every group's name, photo and member count is listed for anyone on Plank Challenge to find and join; who planked today is only shown to the group's members.
  • Your email address is never shown to other people.

Why we use it

Only to run the app for you: signing you in, saving your planks, showing your groups their standings, and sending the notifications you've turned on. Under UK and EU data protection law, our basis is providing the service you asked for (contract). We don't use your data for advertising or profiling, and we don't sell or share it with anyone except the services below.

Services that handle data for us

  • Supabase — our database, file storage and sign-in. Your data is stored on servers in London, United Kingdom.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, and delivering notifications.
  • Google — Sign in with Google, if you use it.
  • An email delivery service — to send your sign-in codes and group invitations.

Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. That permanently deletes your profile, photo, planks, memberships, alerts and notification tokens, and revokes Sign in with Apple for the app. Groups you created stay for the people in them, passed to the member who has been there longest. Copies in our provider's backups disappear within a short time.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by emailing petras.leonardas@gmail.com. If you're in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Children

Plank Challenge is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep data about them. If you think a child has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change matters.
