Support

Questions, a bug, or something not working? Email petras.leonardas@gmail.com and say which iPhone and iOS version you're on.

Not getting notifications? Check Settings → Notifications → Plank Challenge, and Profile → Notifications in the app.
Can't find a group? Tap Search on the Groups tab: every group is listed A–Z, and typing part of its name narrows the list. Can't see it? Ask the owner for an invite.
Deleting your account In the app: Profile → Delete account. Everything of yours is deleted; groups you created stay for their members.
